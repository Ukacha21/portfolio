## 7. Application-Ready Quotes & Phrasing

### For "Inspiration":
> *"After the 2023 Turkey earthquakes, construction material costs rose by 108.7%. Simultaneously, 116–210 million tonnes of rubble — the equivalent of unprocessed raw material — was being expensively cleared and landfilled. The waste and the need existed side by side, completely disconnected."*
> — Source: TÜİK (2023); UNOPS (2023)

### For "How It Works" — geopolymer:
> *"Alkali-activated geopolymers cure at ambient temperature — no kiln, no heat — achieving 15–25 MPa compressive strength within 24–48 hours. The binding chemistry is identical to that of the new generation of low-carbon construction binders now used in infrastructure projects worldwide."*
> — Source: Davidovits (2011); Provis & van Deventer (2014)

### For "What Sets It Apart":
> *"No field-deployable system currently exists that combines on-site rubble crushing, geopolymer mixing, and compression in a single unit. Rubble stays on site; new bricks need not be imported; and material costs — which rose 108.7% in Turkey post-earthquake — are reduced to only the energy and binder required."*
