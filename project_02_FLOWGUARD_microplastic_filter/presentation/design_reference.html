▸ Flow Physics — Why 70mm?
Velocity reduction calculation:

Typical drain pump flow: Q = 20 L/min = 3.33×10⁻⁴ m³/s

At 22mm hose bore:
v₁ = Q/A = 0.88 m/s

At 64mm housing bore:
v₂ = Q/A = 0.10 m/s

Velocity reduction = 8.8× slower
Fibres fall out of suspension below ~0.15 m/s.
The 64mm ID achieves this comfortably. ✅

Pressure drop across housing:
Bernoulli + minor losses ≈ ΔP < 0.05 bar
Well within pump tolerance (faults above 0.3 bar back-pressure). ✅
⚠ SolidWorks note: Model the housing as a single extruded body. The port stubs are boss extrusions on the end faces. The internal bore is a cut extrude from each end meeting in the middle. Use Shell feature (3mm thickness) for the cylindrical walls.

💡 SolidWorks modelling order (recommended):
1. Housing body (P01) — shell feature, 3mm wall
2. Port stubs — boss extrude on end faces
3. O-ring groove — revolve cut on stub OD
4. Piston (P04) — revolve, add thread cosmetic feature
5. Ejector cap (P05) — revolve
6. Mesh basket (P03) — surface body or thin-shell revolve
7. Latch collar (P02) — extrude + cut for bore steps
8. Assembly — mate all parts, add flow simulation
⚠ Critical tolerances to set:
• Piston to housing: H7/f6 fit (sliding clearance ~0.05mm)
• Mesh OD to housing ID: 0.5mm radial clearance (allows easy drop-in)
• O-ring groove: squeeze 15–20% of cross-section (standard EPDM)
• Latch tab snap: 0.3mm interference on the catch — audible click
• Thread engagement: minimum 3 full turns (M63×2 → 6mm engagement)

§8 SolidWorks Flow Simulation Setup
Boundary Conditions:
• Inlet: Volume flow rate = 3.33×10⁻⁴ m³/s (20 L/min)
• Outlet: Static pressure = 0 Pa (atmospheric)
• Walls: No-slip condition
• Fluid: Water at 60°C (hot wash)
• Turbulence: k-ε model

Goals to track:
• Velocity at mid-chamber cross-section (target: <0.15 m/s)
• Pressure drop inlet→outlet (target: <0.05 bar)
• Surface pressure on latch tabs (target: <0.3 bar)
Static Stress Analysis (FEA):
• Fix housing body at port face
• Apply 0.5 bar internal pressure to chamber walls
• Apply 20N point load on each latch tab (finger press force)
• Check: factor of safety >3 on PP (yield = 30 MPa)

Expected results:
• Max stress on latch tab ~8 MPa → FoS = 3.75 ✅
• Housing wall stress ~2 MPa → FoS = 15 ✅
• No yielding anywhere at operating conditions ✅
