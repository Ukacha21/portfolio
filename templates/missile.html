Folding Canard & Fin Deployment Mechanism

Precision guided munitions require minimal volumetric footprint during canister or tube storage, with instantaneous aerodynamic deployment upon launch. Designed parameterized folding canard surfaces actuated through dedicated flap holder shafts (a7_4_flap_holder_shaft) seated within recessed longitudinal slots on the missile skin.

  • Kinematic Linkage: Integrated pivot pins allowing 90° rotational deployment outward from fuselage slots.
  • Locking Channels: Mechanical stops to sustain high dynamic air pressure during supersonic/transonic flight.
  • Symmetrical 4-Axis Array: Independent quad-canard control surfaces for high pitch, yaw, and roll maneuverability.

Aerodynamic Seeker Radome & Guidance Nose

The forward missile segment (a1_head) features a golden tangent ogive radome profile optimized for reduced drag coefficient at varying angles of attack while allocating adequate internal volume for target guidance sensor optics and electro-optical / RF seeker hardware.

  • Tangent Ogive Geometry: Low wave drag profile transitioning seamlessly into the cylindrical body section.
  • Interlocking Shoulder Joint: Concentric mating ring securing the guidance dome to the middle payload section.
  • Sensor Packaging: Designed to accommodate electro-optical/infrared (EO/IR) or laser seeker payload heads.

Exhibition Cutaway & Internal Demonstration View

To fulfill AI Armaments’ objective for their tech fair showcase in the Netherlands, the 3D model incorporates a translucent cutaway mid-fuselage section (a2_a3_middle). This allows visitors and defense industry evaluators to directly inspect the inner mechanical actuators, cylindrical chambers, and linkages in full operation.

  • Visual Transparency: Parametric cutaway rendering showcasing the mechanical heart of the weapon.
  • Modular Mid-Chamber: Adaptable for loitering munition payload bays or solid rocket propellant simulation.
  • High-Resolution Rendering: Calibrated with studio lighting, metallic shaders, and contrast depth for trade fair exhibits.

Tail Section & Rear Propulsion Stabilization

The aft section (a4_a5_back_and_tail) houses the rocket nozzle cavity and secondary folding stabilization fins. Modeled with high-tolerance concentric alignments to ensure aerodynamic stability across turbulent flight phases and rocket motor ignition.

  • Cruciform Tail Fins: Aerodynamically balanced stabilization surfaces providing passive directional stability.
  • Propulsion Interface: Rear exit nozzle profile modeled for rocket motor exhaust flow dynamics.
  • Structural Rigidity: Integrated internal bulkheads to absorb axial launch accelerations.
