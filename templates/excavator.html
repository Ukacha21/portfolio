Official Engineering Statement (English)

Comprehensive Excavator Design Project: Bridging Theory and Practice

I am proud to announce that, together with my colleagues, we have successfully completed our comprehensive excavator design project within the scope of our Machine Elements course. This journey was a rigorous simulation of real-world engineering challenges.

Throughout the project, we focused intensively on the design and analysis of a 24-ton class excavator, prioritizing these 5 core machine elements:

  • 1- #Shafts: Load, stress, deflection, and fatigue analysis of shafts located in work equipment (Boom, Arm, and Bucket).
  • 2- #Bearings: Load calculations and selection of critical bearing systems, including Rollers, Idler, and Slewing bearings.
  • 3- #Bolts: Strength analysis of bolt groups under critical loads.
  • 4- #Gears: Gearbox design tailored to specific torque requirements.
  • 5- #Hydraulics: Optimization of hydraulic cylinders and circuits for lifting and digging forces.
  • #Others: Verification of structural integrity using ANSYS (FEA) and component selection from industrial standard catalogs.

Key Outcomes: Beyond the technical calculations, this project provided us with invaluable professional insights in System Integration, Engineering Judgment, and Simulation Proficiency.

Resmi Mühendislik Açıklaması (Türkçe)

Kapsamlı Ekskavatör Tasarım Projesi: Teorik Bilgiden Mühendislik Pratiğine

Meslektaşlarımla birlikte, Makine Elemanları dersi kapsamında üzerinde çalıştığımız detaylı ekskavatör tasarım projesini başarıyla tamamladık. Bu süreç, bir makinenin sıfırdan inşasında gerekli olan mühendislik disiplinini deneyimlememiz açısından eşsiz bir fırsat oldu.

Proje süresince 24 ton sınıfı bir ekskavatör için özellikle şu 5 temel makine elemanının detaylı tasarımını ve analizini gerçekleştirdik:

  • 1- #Miller: Bom, Arm ve Kova gibi iş ekipmanlarında bulunan millerin yük, gerilme, sehim ve yorulma analizleri.
  • 2- #Rulmanlar: Alt takım (makaralar, istikamet tekeri) ve kule dönüş rulmanlarının yük hesapları ve ömür analizleri.
  • 3- #Civatalar: Kritik yükler altındaki civata gruplarının mukavemet analizleri.
  • 4- #Dişliler: Tork gereksinimlerine uygun dişli mekanizması tasarımları.
  • 5- #Hidrolik: Kazma ve kaldırma kuvvetlerini optimize eden hidrolik silindir ve devre hesaplamaları.
  • #Diğerleri: Tüm bu sistemlerin ANSYS ile yapısal analizlerinin #FEA doğrulanması ve endüstriyel standartlara uygun katalog seçimleri.

TECHNICAL CALCULATIONS

The 5 Core Machine Element Subsystems

Analytical mathematical modeling corroborated with ISO standards and ANSYS FEA simulation.

01

Miller (Shafts & Heavy Pivot Pins)

42CrMo4 Alloy Steel · Induction Hardened (58–60 HRC) · Fatigue Safety Factor Sf ≥ 2.2

The front working attachment (Boom, Arm, Bucket) operates under severe asymmetrical torsional and bending loads during high-velocity digging impacts. We performed analytical shear and bending moment calculations ($V, M_b$) across all 10 pivot pins. Deflection limits were strictly enforced ($y_{\text{max}} < 0.001 \cdot L$) to avoid premature bushing scuffing. Structural integrity was verified via ANSYS Mechanical FEA, displaying Von Mises stress contours across every pin under maximum crowd and breakout force.

02

Rulmanlar (Bearings & Undercarriage Rotational Systems)

Kule Dönüş Rulmanı (Slewing Ring) · Track Rollers & Idler · ISO 281 L10h > 12,000 hrs

Calculated the multi-axis load capacity for the massive four-point contact ball Slewing Ring (Kule Dönüş Rulmanı), capable of transmitting axial machine weight, radial side-cutting reactions, and substantial overturning tilting moments ($M_t$). In the undercarriage, track rollers (yürüyüş makaraları) and front idler wheels (istikamet tekeri) were sized using heavy-duty tapered roller bearings with sealed duo-cone floating seals, ensuring L10h operating life exceeding 12,000 hours in harsh abrasive terrain.

03

Civatalar (Preloaded Fastener Assemblies & Bolted Joints)

ISO 898-1 Metric Grade 10.9 & 12.9 · Preload Force Fi · Joint Safety Sp ≥ 1.8

Structural bolt groups securing the slewing bearing to the carbody and revolving frame, counterweight mounting bolts, and track motor fastener groups were analyzed under critical combined tension, shear, and prying forces. Utilizing the VDI 2230 systematic bolted joint calculation guideline, we determined tightening torques ($M_A$), minimum required clamping preload ($F_i = 0.70 \cdot R_{p0.2} \cdot A_s$), and verified joint separation safety factors ($S_p \ge 1.8$) to prevent fatigue failure under fluctuating cyclic digging loads.

Key Fastener Metric: Grade 10.9 & 12.9 quenched & tempered high-tensile alloy fasteners with zinc-flake anti-corrosion coating; calculated preload guarantees zero gap formation under maximum counterweight dynamic pitching acceleration.
04

Dişliler (Planetary Swing & Travel Reducers)

20MnCr5 Case-Carburized (58–62 HRC) · AGMA Root Bending & Hertzian Pitting Verified

Engineered compact multi-stage planetary gearbox systems (Sun gear, Planet gears, Ring gear) for both the high-torque swing drive and track crawler drive motors. Calculations incorporated AGMA and ISO 6336 standards to guarantee tooth root bending endurance ($\sigma_F \le \sigma_{FP}$) and Hertzian surface contact fatigue resistance ($\sigma_H \le \sigma_{HP}$) against pitting. Case-carburized alloy steel 20MnCr5 was specified for maximum surface hardness and core toughness.

05

Hidrolik (High-Pressure Hydraulic Cylinders & Circuit)

350 Bar Working Pressure · 149 kN Breakout Force · Euler Column Buckling nk ≥ 3.5

Optimized the hydraulic cylinder geometry for dual Boom cylinders, Arm crowd cylinder, and Bucket cylinder to deliver the required 149 kN breakout force. Calculated required piston diameter ($D$) and rod diameter ($d$) at 350 bar operating pressure. Conducted comprehensive Euler column buckling stability checks ($n_k = \frac{\pi^2 E I}{L_k^2 \cdot F} \ge 3.5$) for fully extended chrome-plated piston rods under maximum crowd force, along with Lamé thick-walled hoop stress calculations for the forged cylinder barrels.

Endüstriyel Katalog & Standart Parça Seçimi

SKF · Rothe Erde · Parker · Rexroth · DIN & ISO Standards Compliance
