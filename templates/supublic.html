SUPUBLIC — AUTOMATED DRINKING WATER KIOSK Parametric SolidWorks Sheet Metal Architecture, CNC Bending & Physical Prototype Fabrication for Municipal Clean Water Access

SuPublic is a pioneering social and environmental initiative originated in Konya, Turkey, dedicated to eliminating single-use plastic bottled water waste. By mapping clean, free drinking water fountains across Turkey and internationally, SuPublic enables citizens to refill their reusable water bottles on the go. To advance this mission toward automated municipal deployment, Ukacha was contracted to engineer the complete SolidWorks parametric sheet metal enclosure, unrolled laser-cut patterns, press brake bending allowances, structural mounting flanges, and ergonomic bottle refill niche for the working kiosk prototype.
