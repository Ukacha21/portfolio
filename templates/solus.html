Occupational Health Crisis

The Problem: Silent Hyperthermia in the Informal Sector

Outdoor labourers cross life-threatening physiological thresholds minutes before symptoms appear. Existing consumer technology fails the workers who need it most.

The Physiological Blind Spot

In exertional heat stroke (EHS), core temperature spikes exponentially under heavy metabolic load and solar irradiance. However, classical symptoms—cognitive disorientation, loss of sweating, and ataxia—only manifest when core temperature has already breached 40.0°C (104°F).

At that juncture, cellular necrosis and circulatory collapse occur in minutes, resulting in an untreated mortality rate up to 80%. Supervisors managing 20 to 50 field hands cannot visually identify internal hyperthermia through visual observation alone until a worker physically collapses.

“Agriculture is projected to account for 60% of all lost working hours due to heat stress by 2030, with agricultural workers facing up to 35 times greater mortality risk than other sectors.”

— International Labour Organization (ILO) / WMO Joint Report

Why Smartwatches & Consumer Wearables Fail

  • ✕
    Prohibitive Capital Cost ($300–$800): Devices like Kenzen, Apple Watch, or Garmin cost more than an informal worker's monthly wages, making fleet-wide employer procurement economically impossible.
  • ✕
    Daily Battery Charging & Connectivity Dependency: Remote construction zones and rural tea, sugarcane, and cotton fields lack reliable electrical charging infrastructure or cellular data pairing.
  • ✕
    Optical PPG Sensor Failure Under Grime & Perspiration: Skin-contact PPG sensors suffer severe artifact noise and accuracy degradation when exposed to mud, mineral dust, sunscreen, and profuse perspiration.
Mechatronic & Chemical Systems

4-Pillar Engineering Architecture

How SOLUS achieves battery-free biophysical monitoring and long-range acoustic supervisory alerting.

01
🧪

Microencapsulated Leuco Dye System

A proprietary multicomponent thermochromic chemistry comprising an electron-donating leuco dye, a proton donor (developer), and a long-chain fatty ester solvent microencapsulated in melamine resin (2–5 µm capsules).

  • Activation Threshold: Calibrated to 38.5°C skin-equivalent (±0.5°C)
  • Phase Mechanism: Reversible solid-to-liquid phase transition
  • Environmental Shield: Sealed against UV photodegradation and sweat pH
02
⚡

Passive Conductive Phase-Change Trace

A screen-printed flexible silver-polymer conductive trace integrated on the patch boundary. In the crystalline low-temperature state, the circuit remains open. As the microcapsules transition into their liquid isotropic state at 38.5°C, thermal expansion bridges the contact gap.

  • Zero Worker Battery: No power cell or silicon MCU on worker's body
  • Switching Precision: Sub-second mechanical response upon reaching threshold
  • Trace Substrate: 50 µm flexible bio-compatible polyester backing
03
📡

433 MHz ISM-Band Alert Transmitter

A micro-RF burst generator operating in the license-free 433.92 MHz ISM band. When the conductive trace closes, an ultra-low-current burst transmits up to 50 metres to the supervisor's receiver wristband.

  • Noise Penetration: Penetrates heavy machinery, engine roar, and wind
  • Supervisor Wristband: Solar-rechargeable, emits 85 dB audible alarm & vibration
  • Zero Pairing: Autonomous multi-channel broadcasts without Bluetooth pairing
04
🩹

Hypoallergenic Sweat-Venting Substrate

Engineered with micro-perforated medical-grade polyurethane film coated with a hypoallergenic silicone gel adhesive. Maintains steady dermal adhesion under heavy perspiration while allowing natural evaporative cooling.

  • Dermal Safety: ISO 10993 cytotoxicity and skin irritation tested
  • Shift Durability: Withstands 12–14 hours of extreme outdoor friction
  • Painless Removal: Leaves zero adhesive residue upon end-of-shift peel
Visual Ergonomics

The 3-Stage Colorimetric Warning Mechanism

Standardized according to ISO 3864 occupational safety colour coding for instant, language-independent visual assessment under bright daylight.

STAGE 1: GREEN
< 37.5°C Core Equivalent

Baseline Safe Operating Zone

The leuco dye remains in its opaque green microcrystalline state. Autonomic thermoregulation is functioning normally through standard cutaneous vasodilation and sweat evaporation.

Mandated Action: Continue standard shift cadence with routine hydration breaks.
STAGE 2: AMBER
37.5°C – 38.4°C

Pre-Stroke Caution Window

Partial solvent liquefaction shifts the optical spectrum to bright amber. The worker has accumulated significant heat strain and cardiovascular compensation is approaching threshold.

Mandated Action: Immediate 15-minute shaded rest, electrolyte intake, supervisor monitoring.
STAGE 3: RED
≥ 38.5°C Alert Window

Critical Intervention Window

Complete isotropic phase shift to vivid crimson. Conductive trace closes, firing the 433 MHz RF alarm pulse. Irreversible heat stroke (≥40°C) is imminent within minutes without rapid cooling.

Mandated Action: Cease all labor immediately. Move to active shade. Apply ice/cold water slurry to neck and groin.

Verification & Empirical Testing

James Dyson Award Prototype Iteration Log

Demonstrating honest iterative engineering development and experimental physical validation.

ITERATION 01

Commercial Thermochromic Pigment Acquisition & Water Bath Calibration

Procured commercial leuco dye thermochromic pigments formulated for 37°C body heat activation. Conducted controlled thermal calibration using precision warm water immersion baths (36.0°C to 40.0°C in 0.5°C increments).

Challenge Identified: Commercial off-the-shelf pigments exhibited broad transition hysteretic slopes (~3.5°C band), triggering color fade prematurely at 36.2°C.
ITERATION 02

Microencapsulated Leuco Formulation & 3-Stage Layering

Synthesized layered test strips utilizing high-purity methyl stearate solvent cores to sharpen the phase transition temperature curve to ±0.5°C around the 38.5°C threshold. Tested three distinct chromatic zones (Green, Amber, Red) on medical tape substrates.

Result: Achieved clear visual distinction between 37.0°C (Green), 37.8°C (Amber), and 38.5°C (Red) under direct outdoor 100,000 lux sunlight.
ITERATION 03

Autonomous 433 MHz RF Trigger & Supervisor Breadboard Receiver

Constructed independent RF verification hardware using an HC-12 / FS1000A 433 MHz transceiver module pair. Designed conductive silver micro-switch contacts triggered mechanically upon the dye layer's phase expansion, successfully tripping an 85 dB piezo buzzer on a prototype supervisor wristband over 50m line-of-sight.

Result: Confirmed 85 dB acoustic penetration through recorded noise of diesel tractors and combine harvesters.
ITERATION 04

Field Trials Roadmap: Turkey & India Agricultural Co-Ops

Planned human subject validation trials with agricultural cooperatives in Çukurova, Turkey and Maharashtra, India to evaluate sweat immersion adhesion across varied dermal phototypes, relative humidity conditions (30% to 90%), and physical work exertion.

Next Phase: CE PPE Regulation (EU) 2016/425 compliance pathway for occupational personal protective gear.
