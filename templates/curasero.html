CURA SERO (AVICENNA) Biomimetic Wet-Surface Wound Closure Strip with Micro-Tension Ratchet & Sustained Antimicrobial Release

In rural low-resource clinics, soft-tissue lacerations go untreated because traditional sutures require sterile instruments, local anaesthesia, and trained clinicians. Standard adhesive strips (Steri-Strips) fail catastrophically on sweaty or bleeding skin (>80% adhesion loss). CURA SERO replaces invasive suturing with gecko-inspired hexagonal micro-suction pillars (van der Waals adhesion) that grip wet tissue firmly without glue, a micro-ratcheting mechanical chassis delivering 3-0 suture-equivalent tension, and an integrated 48–72h chlorhexidine antimicrobial hydrogel matrix deployable in under 60 seconds with zero clinical training.

Engineering Architecture

The 3-Layer Bio-Mechanical Triad

Why chemical glues fail on wet skin, why sutures cause puncture scarring, and how three integrated mechanical layers solve last-mile closure in 60 seconds.

LAYER 01
🦎

Gecko Biomimetic Micro-Suction Base

10–50 µm Hexagonal Pillars · Medical Silicone (USP Class VI)

Inspired by gecko setae spatula mechanics, the base substrate is cast in medical-grade biocompatible silicone with high-density hexagonal mushroom-capped micro-pillars. Instead of chemical pressure-sensitive adhesives that disperse in water, the micro-pillars displace moisture thin-films and generate massive intermolecular van der Waals attraction across microscopic skin asperities.

  • Wet Shear Adhesion: Retains >85% tensile hold on sweat, seawater, and blood.
  • Trauma-Free Removal: Directional peel release leaves zero epidermal tears or adhesive residue.
  • Hypoallergenic: Zero cyanoacrylate solvent toxicity or contact dermatitis.
LAYER 02
⚙️

Bilateral Micro-Ratcheting Chassis

0.8 N/cm² Controlled Force · 3-0 Suture Gauge Equivalent

Two rigid polymer carrier rails interlock through a high-precision miniature ratcheting rack. As the community health worker pinches the lateral tabs, the ratchet draws wound margins smoothly into anatomical approximation. Non-reversing ratchet teeth prevent slippage under patient movement while an internal mechanical torque stop prevents excessive compression necrosis.

  • Non-Invasive: Zero needle puncture wounds; eliminates track scarring and puncture infections.
  • 60-Second Application: Requires no sterile field setup, needle holders, or suture forceps.
  • Uniform Distribution: Eliminates point-stress concentrations seen in interrupted stitches.
LAYER 03
💧

Antimicrobial Hydrogel Elution Reservoir

0.5% Chlorhexidine Gluconate · Crosslinked PVA Matrix

The top barrier layer consists of a semi-permeable polyurethane film backing a crosslinked polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) hydrogel loaded with 0.5% chlorhexidine gluconate. When wound exudate penetrates the matrix, it triggers sustained diffusion of the broad-spectrum antiseptic across the closure gap for 48–72 hours, preventing bacterial colonisation in unsanitary field environments.

  • Broad-Spectrum Bactericidal: Active against Gram-positive, Gram-negative, and MRSA.
  • Low Resistance Risk: Displaces prophylactic oral antibiotic overuse in remote outposts.
  • Moist Healing Bed: Accelerates re-epithelialisation and reduces keloid scar tissue formation.
Evidence Base & Global Need

The Last-Mile Surgical Vacuum

Why untreated lacerations lead to chronic infection, sepsis, and permanent disability in low-resource settings.

2 Billion
People Lack Access to Basic Surgery

According to the Lancet Commission on Global Surgery (2030), over two billion people live without access to timely surgical care. In rural Sub-Saharan Africa, only 1–5% of the population have access to surgical facilities, leaving soft-tissue trauma untreated.

Source: Meara, J. G. et al. (2015). Global Surgery 2030. The Lancet.
>80%
Adhesion Loss for Standard Strips in Wet Field Conditions

Clinical testing confirms that standard medical pressure-sensitive adhesives (acrylic and silicone PSAs used in Steri-Strips) lose more than 80% of their shear strength when applied to moist or perspiring skin, leading to premature wound dehiscence.

Source: Chen, X. et al. (2020). Wet adhesion in biomimetic adhesives. WIREs Nanomed.
Van der Waals
Gecko Biophysics vs. Chemical Adhesion

Gecko setae maintain functional adhesion on wet surfaces because their microscopic fibrillar geometry maximises intimate molecular contact, generating intermolecular van der Waals forces without relying on chemical tack or moisture-sensitive solvents.

Source: Autumn, K. et al. (2002). Evidence for van der Waals adhesion. PNAS.
