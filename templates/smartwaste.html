SMARTWASTE Autonomous In-Bin Material Sorter, Laser ToF Telemetry & 3:1 Volume Compactor

Municipal waste logistics consume 70–80% of solid waste operational budgets worldwide, yet trucks still follow rigid static routes — collecting half-empty bins while uncompressed plastic and cardboard waste fills containers with 70% trapped ambient air. SMARTWASTE is an autonomous retrofittable IoT smart-lid module engineered for Konya Selçuklu municipality. It unites millimetre-precise VCSEL laser ToF fill telemetry, a high-speed bimodal inductive & optical sorting gate, and an electromechanical 1.2 kN lead-screw compactor to triple storage capacity at an under-$80 unit BOM.

System Engineering & Hardware Design

The Three-Pillar Retrofit Architecture

How SMARTWASTE integrates millimeter-grade photonics, electromechanical sorting gates, and mechanical compression into an unpowered container lid.

🔬
PILLAR 01 · PHOTONICS

Laser Time-of-Flight (ToF) Fill Scanning

Legacy smart bins rely on ultrasonic ping sensors, which produce systematic errors inside waste containers due to acoustic multipath echoes off irregular trash heaps, steam condensation, and odor gases. SMARTWASTE deploys a waterproof 940 nm VCSEL laser ToF sensor calculating photon transit times with millimeter precision regardless of trash surface color or container humidity.

  • Sensor IC: VL53L1X long-range ToF module (up to 4.0 m range).
  • Optical Port: Hydrophobic anti-dust sapphire glass window.
  • Sampling: Adaptive 3-point conical sweep to map peaked center mounds.
🔀
PILLAR 02 · ACTUATION

Bimodal Inductive & Optical Sorting Gate

Eliminates expensive hyperspectral cameras by combining a high-sensitivity inductive eddy-current loop with dual-wavelength IR reflectance phototransistors. When an item slides down the inlet tray, conductive metals (cans, foil, aerosol tins) trip an immediate inductive frequency shift, while non-conductive polymers are measured for surface diffuse reflectance, actuating a high-torque waterproof servo chute.

  • Actuation: Waterproof metal-gear servo (25 kg·cm stall torque, 0.12s sweep).
  • Inductive Sensitivity: Detects aluminium pull-tabs down to 0.4 mm thickness.
  • Fail-Safe: Default return spring directs unknown objects to general stream.
⚙️
PILLAR 03 · MECHANICS

In-Situ 3:1 Motorised Mechanical Press

Lightweight compressible waste (PET bottles, beverage cartons, Amazon boxes) prematurely fills municipal containers with 70% trapped ambient air. Rather than attempting to compress rigid metal or glass, SMARTWASTE isolates the compressible polymer stream into Chamber B, where an automated lead-screw piston delivers 1.2 kN of compaction thrust, reducing item volume by 3:1 and tripling effective bin life.

  • Drive Mechanism: Heavy-duty Tr12×3 trapezoidal lead screw with bronze nut.
  • Motor Unit: Planetary-geared 12V DC motor with current-sensing stall limit.
  • Overload Protection: Automatic current trip and reverse stroke if hard jam detected.
📡
PILLAR 04 · EMBEDDED IoT

Ultra-Low Power IoT Fleet Backbone

To operate off-grid for 180+ days on a small rechargeable lithium pack, the ESP32 microcontroller spends 99.8% of its lifespan in Deep Sleep drawing only 15 µA. The processor wakes up instantly via a PIR deposit trigger or timer interrupt, takes a 50 ms distance measurement, and connects to Wi-Fi/cellular only when fill exceeds the 80% threshold or for daily health heartbeats.

  • Microcontroller: ESP32 Dual-Core Xtensa LX6 with ULP coprocessor.
  • Battery Life: 180+ days on dual 18650 Li-ion cells (7.4V, 3400 mAh).
  • Protocols: Lightweight MQTT / HTTPS payload to municipal dispatch API.
