Disaster Realities & The Disconnection Paradox

The Absurdity of Post-Earthquake Logistics

Millions of tonnes of rubble are expensively trucked to landfills while earthquake survivors await unaffordable imported masonry bricks.

🇹🇷 Direct First-Hand Observation · Türkiye February 2023
"As an international mechatronics engineering student living in Türkiye during the 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquakes, I witnessed the immense humanitarian crisis up close. In the aftermath, I watched two simultaneous, conflicting realities: millions of tonnes of rubble sitting in ruins, costing millions of dollars to haul away, while devastated communities sat without shelter because new bricks had spiked over 108% in cost. The waste and the need were sitting side by side, completely disconnected. Rubble is not waste — it is pre-mined, pre-quarried raw material waiting to be rebound."

Primary Comminution & Classification

Single-Toggle Jaw Crusher with Vibratory Rebar Sieve

Earthquake rubble contains unpredictable concrete lumps, fragmented mortar, and embedded wire mesh. The RENOVO jaw crusher utilizes high-manganese cast steel jaw plates (13% Mn) with an adjustable eccentric toggle mechanism capable of handling up to 150 mm feed lumps.

Throughput Capacity: 50–80 kg/hour (continuous)
Input Feed Size: Up to 150 mm concrete offcuts
Discharge Particle Size: 5–20 mm optimal RCA aggregate
Drive Mechanism: 1.5 kW 24V DC motor with 600 mm inertia flywheel
Manual Mode: Counterweighted dual-hand crank for flywheel assist
Integrated Sieve: 20 mm aperture woven steel screen with gravity chute
Why 5–20 mm aggregate matters: Published geotechnical literature confirms that 5–20 mm aggregate optimizes geopolymer paste surface wetting, preventing voids while retaining maximum interlock friction under compressive loading.

Stoichiometric Matrix Homogenization

Horizontal Helical Ribbon Mixing Drum

Unlike virgin river sand, recycled concrete aggregate has high surface angularity and erratic micro-porosity. Standard gravity drum mixers fail because paste sticks to the walls. RENOVO employs a positive-displacement counter-current helical ribbon blade shaft that forces viscous geopolymer paste into every micro-fissure.

Batch Capacity: 12 kg per cycle (~5 full bricks)
Mixing Time: 150–180 seconds per batch
Volumetric Ratio: 75% crushed aggregate : 25% geopolymer paste
Blade Geometry: Double-flight opposing helical ribbon paddles
Drive: 0.5 kW worm-gear reduction motor / manual crank
Discharge: Underside sliding gate with direct chute to mold
Self-Wetting Rheology: Sodium silicate solution acts as a chemical dispersant, ensuring aggregate is thoroughly lubricated before compression without drowning the matrix in excess water.

High-Pressure Densification

10–15 MPa Hydraulic Compaction Press & Ejection Rig

Geopolymer bonding requires rapid compaction to expel air pockets and force aluminosilicate oligomers into intimate atomic contact with aggregate silica facets. A 4-column guided hydraulic ram exerts up to 15 MPa across the standard ISO mold face, initiating immediate mechanical lock.

Compression Force: 250–370 kN (10–15 MPa specific pressure)
Mold Cavity: ISO Standard 228 × 108 × 76 mm
Tie-Rod Columns: 4× Ø40 mm chrome-plated 4140 alloy steel
Ram Stroke: 120 mm double-acting hydraulic cylinder
Cycle Time: ~90–120 seconds per finished brick
Ejection Mechanism: Synchronized lower ram lift to roller outfeed tray
Immediate Green Strength: Upon hydraulic decompression, the dense green brick possesses enough cohesive strength to be lifted immediately and stacked without slumping.

Materials Science & Chemical Kinetics

Why Geopolymers Succeed Where Portland Cement Fails

Recycled concrete aggregate absorbs 4–8× more water than virgin stone, causing ordinary Portland cement to crack. Alkali-activated geopolymers turn this weakness into a chemical asset.

01

Alkali Dissolution

Sodium silicate ($Na_2SiO_3$) and sodium hydroxide ($NaOH$) hydrolyze the glassy vitreous phase of industrial fly ash and slag, rapidly liberating free $[SiO_4]^{4-}$ and $[AlO_4]^{5-}$ tetrahedra into aqueous suspension.

(Si₂O₅,Al₂O₂)ₙ + OH⁻ + H₂O ➔ Si(OH)₄ + Al(OH)₄⁻
02

Oligomer Condensation

The dissolved aluminate and silicate complexes condense into mobile oligomers (sialate $[–Si–O–Al–O–]$ chains). These oligomers wet the jagged micro-pores of crushed concrete aggregate, forming covalent bonding bridges.

n(Si–O–Al–O) ➔ (–Si–O–Al–O–Si–O–)ₙ Poly(sialate-siloxo)
03

Ambient 3D Polycondensation

As water is expelled during 10–15 MPa hydraulic compaction, the oligomers polymerize into a continuous rigid 3D aluminosilicate framework. It cures fully at 20–35°C without autoclaves, steam chambers, or kilns.

Empirical Validation & Dyson Proof of Work

From Cup-Scale Chemistry to Field Engineering

The James Dyson Award values verifiable iterative progress over conceptual renders. RENOVO was developed through benchtop chemistry and structural load testing.

01
Chemistry Verification

Paper-Cup Geopolymer Hardening Test

Mixed technical-grade sodium silicate ($Na_2SiO_3$) with class F fly ash and GGBS in room temperature ambient conditions. Demonstrated rock-solid hardening within 24 hours without external heat.

02
Mechanical Comminution

Hammer-Crushed Concrete Aggregate Test

Demolished a salvaged C20 concrete paving slab with manual sledgehammer blows, sieving fractions between 5 mm and 20 mm. Mixed with geopolymer slurry at 75:25 volumetric ratio and pressed into a tabletop test mold.

03
Qualitative Load Testing

Impact Resistance & Bodyweight Hold

After 48-hour ambient cure, the recycled rubble block supported full human bodyweight without deflection and resisted repeated direct hammer strikes without brittle shearing.

Humanitarian Logistics & Operational Scale

The First 72 Hours: Air-Freight to Permanent Shelter

Bridging the critical gap between emergency tent distribution and long-term municipal reconstruction.

PHASE 1 · 0–72 HOURS

Air-Freight Sortie Deployment

A single C-130 Hercules cargo aircraft can transport four complete RENOVO press units alongside 8 tonnes of dry alkaline activator concentrate directly to forward humanitarian runways.

  • Each module weighs <180 kg for manual two-person carry
  • Unloaded and assembled in <90 minutes on rough terrain
  • Zero specialized cranes or heavy lifting rigging needed
PHASE 2 · DAYS 3–14

On-Site Rubble Processing & Clearing

Rather than waiting for excavators to clear streets, volunteer crews sort surface rubble directly into the jaw crusher hopper, clearing access corridors while producing raw bricks.

  • 240 standard bricks produced per 8-hour shift per machine
  • ~960 kg of street-blocking debris eliminated daily
  • Ambient curing stacks require zero tarping or water spraying
PHASE 3 · DAYS 14+

Core Shelter Masonry Construction

Local masons lay the 228 × 108 × 76 mm bricks using conventional mortar or geopolymer slurry bed joints. Families build permanent, earthquake-resilient masonry cores.

  • Standard ISO brick geometry eliminates mason retraining
  • 15–25 MPa compressive strength supports single-storey loadbearing roofs
  • Permanent reconstruction begins weeks before commercial supplies return
