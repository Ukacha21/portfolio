SENDIT Autonomous Airport Security Item Mail-Home Kiosk & Asset Recovery Platform

Every year, over 100 million items are confiscated or voluntarily surrendered at airport security screening worldwide — valued at £12–£40 each. Travellers face a high-stress dilemma with only 30–90 seconds before holding up the lane: abandon valuable perfume, pocket knives, power banks, or tools in a bin, or miss their flight returning to check-in. SENDIT is an automated, self-service security kiosk that packages, classifies, labels, weighs, and dispatches surrendered items to the passenger's home address in under 120 seconds — requiring zero airport staff, zero airport capex, and generating new revenue from previously wasted customer stress.

The 8-Module Kiosk Hardware Architecture

Engineered for continuous 24/7/365 terminal operation. Slim 45 cm × 30 cm × 160 cm footprint fitting standard security queuing lanes with zero structural building alterations.

MODULE 01 Display

15.6" Industrial Touchscreen Display

1920×1080 Full HD optically bonded capacitive touch panel with anti-glare scratch-resistant coating (IK08 impact rated). Operates reliably with gloved fingers.

MODULE 02 Vision AI

AI Classification Camera (MobileNet V3)

5MP wide-angle CMOS optics with localized LED ring lighting. Runs MobileNet V3 inference in <1.8s to identify 12 TSA categories and check courier prohibited-hazmat rules.

MODULE 03 Compute

Edge SBC & Hardware Watchdog

Raspberry Pi 4/5 (4GB RAM) with solid-state eMMC/A2 storage, passive aluminium heatsink, and dual hardware watchdogs ensuring instant auto-recovery without technician visits.

MODULE 04 Payment

Contactless PCI-PTS Payment Terminal

SumUp Pro embedded payment reader behind the bezel. Supports Apple Pay, Google Pay, Visa, Mastercard, and international bank cards with PCI-DSS Level 1 encryption.

MODULE 05 Logistics

Secure Anti-Tamper Courier Drop-Bin

Internal 60-litre steel lockbox with a one-way rotating drum chute. Features dual infrared break-beam fill sensors alerting dispatch when capacity reaches 85%.

MODULE 06 Sealing

Impulse Heat-Strip Sealer (12V)

12V 40W nichrome wire element actuated by a solid-state relay. Automatically heat-welds heavy-duty polymer mailer bags in 2.2 seconds with zero passenger burn risk.

MODULE 07 Packaging

Gravity-Fed 3-Tier Bag Dispenser

Modular spring-loaded cassette holding 150 padded bubble envelopes, 75 small boxes, and 40 medium flat-pack boxes. Refillable in under 2 minutes by airport handlers.

MODULE 08 Printing

Industrial Direct Thermal Label Printer

Zebra ZD421 / DYMO 550 producing 203 dpi courier-grade 4"×6" synthetic waterproof labels in <3.5 seconds. Uses ink-free thermal transfer ribbons.
