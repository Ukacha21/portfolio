ROS NOETIC AUTONOMOUS MOBILE ROBOT Dual-Core ESP32 Kinematics, High-Throughput Wi-Fi TCP/IP Bridge & Gazebo/RViz Digital Twin Trajectory Odometry

An advanced autonomous mobile robotics platform designed, modeled, and physically built in collaborative partnership with Mechatronics Engineer Mehmet Çakaroğlu under project advisor Dr. Öğr. Üyesi Okan Uyar. The robot establishes a seamless, low-latency bi-directional wireless communication bridge (Wi-Fi TCP/IP socket) between high-level autonomous trajectory planning nodes running in ROS Noetic Ninjemys (Ubuntu/WSL) and low-level microsecond hardware actuation running on a dual-core ESP32. Meeting rigorous university evaluation benchmarks with a perfect 100/100 score, the vehicle demonstrated closed-loop odometry, hardware interrupt quadrature tick counting, 6-DOF IMU drift zeroing, and flawless execution of precision circular orbit and 10-meter out-and-back arena challenges.

Autonomous Arena Challenges & Benchmark Tests

How the vehicle satisfied every stringent criteria across Vize (Terminal Hardware Qualification) and Final (ROS1 TCP/IP Navigation, Gazebo Simulation & RViz Odometry).

STAGE 1 · VIZE QUALIFICATION

Terminal Controlled Locomotion & Sensor Calibration (100 / 100)

Rigorous hardware verification without high-level ROS middleware. Evaluated deterministic terminal responsiveness, latency, and sensor telemetry streaming:

  • Motion Execution (30 Pts): Forward, backward, right pivot, and left pivot executed with smooth transitions and instant zero-latency safe stopping.
  • Quadrature Encoder Telemetry (25 Pts): Bilateral pulse counting (420 ticks/rev), directional signed velocity derivation, and clean terminal stream.
  • MPU6050 IMU Fusion (25 Pts): Real-time Gyro Z and linear acceleration streaming with a 500-sample startup calibration zeroing gyroscope drift.
  • Mechanical & Driving Stability (20 Pts): Zero-divergence straight-line motion and vibration-free zero-radius pivot rotations.

System Topology & Communications

ROS Noetic & ESP32 TCP/IP Architecture

Dual-level modular separation between high-level Linux ROS nodes and deterministic microsecond microcontroller firmware.

💻

High-Level Architecture (ROS Noetic / Linux WSL)

  • Catkin Workspace: Modular packages robot_description (URDF/Xacro, meshes, RViz) and robot_bringup (launch files, nodes, task drivers).
  • TCP Bridge Node: Custom tcp_bridge.py subscribing to standard /cmd_vel (geometry_msgs/Twist) and converting to binary payloads.
  • Sensory Publisher: Decodes incoming telemetry into ROS topics /wheel_ticks, /imu/data, and broadcasts the /tf coordinate transform tree.
  • Simulation Digital Twin: Fully configured Gazebo world with dynamic unicycle physics, friction coefficients, and RViz trajectory monitors.
⚡

Low-Level Architecture (ESP32 C++ / FreeRTOS)

  • SoftAP Wi-Fi Server: Operates in Access Point mode (SSID: ros_aluzia, IP: 192.168.0.153, Port: 5000).
  • Differential Kinematics: Solves wheel velocities: \(v_L = v - \frac{\omega \cdot L}{2}\), \(v_R = v + \frac{\omega \cdot L}{2}\) mapped to PWM.
  • Hardware Interrupts: External pin change interrupts on GPIO 16, 17, 18, 19 ensure zero missed ticks up to high RPM.
  • Deadman Switch Failsafe: If TCP packets cease for >500ms, the ESP32 locks H-bridge pins to prevent runaway accidents.
