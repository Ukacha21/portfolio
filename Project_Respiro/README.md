# Project 10 — RESPIRO: Biomedical Adaptive Assistive Ventilator
## Status: 🚫 Discontinued (Archived R&D)

> **Archival Notice:** Development and prototyping on Project RESPIRO have been officially **discontinued**. Research and engineering resources have been reallocated to active biomedical hardware and mechatronic systems (VELUM, FLOWGUARD, SOLUS, DENOVA, and RENOVO).

---

### Project Overview (Historical Context)
- **Concept:** Low-cost, high-reliability mechanical ventilator engineered for emergency triage deployment and resource-constrained clinics.
- **Architecture:** Closed-loop pressure and volume PID control with proportional solenoid valving and redundant mechanical overpressure relief.
- **Specifications Explored:** Tidal volume 200–800 mL, PEEP regulation 0–20 cmH2O.
- **Status:** Shelved / Archived.
